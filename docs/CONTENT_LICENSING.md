# Content Licensing — Global Minds Academy

## Policy Summary

Global Minds Academy uses **original content only**. This document defines the rules for content creation, attribution, and permissible use of third-party material.

---

## Original Content Policy

All educational content published on the platform — questions, explanations, lesson text, course descriptions, worked examples, study notes, and resource materials — must be **original works** created by Global Minds Academy staff, contracted educators, or verified contributors who have assigned rights to the platform.

**Prohibited without explicit licensing:**

- Copying questions or passages from NCERT textbooks, Tamil Nadu State Board textbooks, or any other official textbook.
- Reproducing copyrighted practice questions from JEE, NEET, BITSAT, or any examination authority's question papers.
- Copying text, diagrams, or images from competitor platforms (BYJU'S, Vedantu, Physics Wallah, etc.).
- Reproducing copyrighted content from international platforms (Khan Academy, BBC Bitesize, etc.) without their specific license.

---

## Examination Information

Information about examinations (JEE Main, JEE Advanced, NEET UG, BITSAT, CUET, etc.) is derived from **official sources only**:

| Source | URL | Usage |

| JEE Advanced official | https://jeeadv.ac.in | Exam dates, eligibility, pattern |
| NEET UG official | https://neet.nta.ac.in | Exam dates, eligibility, pattern |
| NTA (National Testing Agency) | https://nta.ac.in | Official notifications |
| CBSE official | https://cbse.gov.in | Syllabus references |
| Tamil Nadu School Education | https://www.tnschools.gov.in | TN Board curriculum |

**Verification rule**: All examination dates, cut-offs, eligibility criteria, and syllabus information must cite the official source URL and the date it was verified. Never publish unverified figures.

---

## Attribution Requirements

When using openly licensed third-party material (e.g., Creative Commons):

1. Display the licence name and link (e.g., CC BY 4.0).
2. Credit the original author and source.
3. Note any modifications made.
4. The `Resource` model has `licence`, `attribution`, and `sourceUrl` fields for this purpose.

---

## No Affiliation Claims

Global Minds Academy is an **independent** educational platform. Do not:

- Imply official affiliation with CBSE, NTA, IITs, AIIMS, or any examination authority.
- Use official logos or trademarks of any examination board.
- Represent that results, ranks, or cut-offs are guaranteed.
- Fabricate college rankings, placement statistics, or admission data.

Any partnership or affiliation claim requires written confirmation from the relevant institution before publishing.

---

## User-Generated Content

When teachers, tutors, or content editors submit content through the platform:

- They affirm ownership or appropriate rights to all content they submit.
- They grant Global Minds Academy a non-exclusive licence to display, store, and distribute the content on the platform.
- The platform administrator is responsible for reviewing submitted content for copyright compliance before publishing.

---

## Enforcement

Violation of this policy may result in:

1. Removal of infringing content without notice.
2. Suspension of the contributor's account.
3. Legal action as appropriate.

Report suspected copyright violations to: content@globalmindsacademy.com
